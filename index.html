SPACE JUMPER 3D

Hop the fox across floating platforms. Don't fall in the lava.

Keys: W/↑ forward · A D strafe · SPACE to jump (hold for higher · tap again for double jump)

On iPad: use the on-screen buttons. Tap JUMP twice for a double jump.

Watch out for spikes. Collect coins. Difficulty ramps every level.

Tip: tap the Share icon in Safari → "Add to Home Screen" to play full-screen.
